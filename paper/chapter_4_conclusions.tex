\chapter{Conclusions}

For all the physical systems that were simulated in this work, the results obtained are in agreement with the theoretical predictions. Specifically, the absence of a phase transition in the one-dimensional case was confirmed through the analysis of the shape of the magnetization curve, while the critical temperature was numerically estimated with the help of finite-size scaling analysis for the 2D case finding $T_c^{2D} \approx 2.283 \pm 0.015$ and three dimensional case finding $T_c^{3D} \approx 4.51 \pm 0.05$.

It was noted that the error in the 3D case is significantly higher than in the 2D case, and that it is a direct consequence of the fact that the error for 3D systems scales faster than for 2D systems.

Moreover, the Critical Slowing Down at the critical temperature was observed in the 2D and 3D cases, which is a problem of all the local update algorithms. A possible improvement to the Metropolis-Hastings algorithm and this work could be to implement a cluster update algorithm, such as the Wolff algorithm, which is known to reduce the critical slowing down.
